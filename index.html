ECO TIPS

Small Actions. Big Impact.

Simple habits can make our campus and planet healthier.

💧

Use Reusable Bottles

Reduce single-use plastic by carrying a reusable water bottle.

💡

Save Electricity

Switch off lights, fans and devices when they are not needed.

📄

Reduce Paper Waste

Use digital notes whenever possible and print only when necessary.

🔋

Recycle E-Waste

Never throw batteries and electronics into regular dustbins.
